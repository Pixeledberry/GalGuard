My allergies

Tap everything you need to avoid. Alpha-gal and each allergy are checked side by side. Anything you add is flagged purple in results.

TickedOff reads ingredient lists, so it can miss things. A “free from” claim on a pack never overrides the check. Always read the label and ask your allergist.

My settings

Alpha-gal checking is off. Turn it back on under My allergies to use the sensitivities below.

Alpha-gal sensitivities: things some people with AGS react to and others don’t. For each one choose Don’t eat (flagged red), Avoid (flagged yellow, the default) or Ignore (not flagged, for things you know you don’t react to). Reactions can change, so check with your allergist.

On the web, the first ingredient scan downloads the text reader (about 15 MB) and needs internet. The Android and iPhone apps use the phone’s built-in text reader.

Export / Import Settings

Back up your settings, favorites, history, rating overrides, theme, recipes and shopping list, or restore them on a new device.

Ingredient data comes from Open Food Facts and its sister projects Open Beauty Facts, Open Products Facts and Open Pet Food Facts (crowdsourced) and can be outdated or wrong. “Natural flavors” and some additives don’t reveal their source. This is a screening aid, not medical advice: always read the label and follow your allergist’s guidance.
